# Manujg.com writing reference

Use this for posts and commentary.

- Write as Manuj: practical, evidence-led, direct and specific.
- Do not invent personal reactions or claim that a small set of news items are the most important developments.
- Attribute surveys, forecasts, company claims and policy proposals clearly; distinguish them from interpretation.
- Lead with the purpose. Use short paragraphs, one main idea per sentence, and natural first person only where it adds ownership.
- Avoid corporate tone, generic transitions, “three things stand out”, inflated claims and AI-sounding conclusions.
- Explain why a development matters to the tool, decision or model being discussed.
- For uncertain topics, name the branch or trade-off rather than asserting one outcome.
- Show the complete draft to Manuj before committing any website article.
- Articles belong in posts. Tools and prototypes belong in tools or lab. Cross-link them where useful.
